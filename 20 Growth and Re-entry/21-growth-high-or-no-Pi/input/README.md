_Emily notes_

Data for stationary growth curves was taken from 20260119_stationary file (+Pi) and 20260120 -Pi_growthcurve where the stationary -Pi cultures were grown on the left side of the plate. This data was chosen because it is relatively clean, and is representative of the most common trends seen in these growth curves.

Data for midlog growth curves was taken from 20260103_midlog for +Pi growth, and the lefthand data from the 20260122 pair of midlog -Pi curves. This data was chosen because it was the cleanest, and the data is representative of what is seen across many runs.
